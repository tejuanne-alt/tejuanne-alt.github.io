MemeApp Community Guidelines

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 | Version: 2026-10-v1
Zero Tolerance Notice: Child sexual abuse and exploitation material (CSAM/CSAE) and credible threats of imminent physical harm result in immediate account termination, content eradication, and referral to law enforcement authorities.

1. Prohibited Content Categories

  • Child Sexual Abuse / Exploitation (CSAM/CSAE): Strictly prohibited in all forms without exception.
  • Sexual or Exploitative Content: Non-consensual sexual imagery, explicit sexual media, and sexual solicitation.
  • Hate Speech & Harassment: Content promoting violence, hatred, or degradation against protected characteristics.
  • Threats & Physical Violence: Incitement to violence, glorification of self-harm, or terroristic threats.
  • Bullying & Degradation: Targeted abusive campaigns, shaming, or malicious humiliation.
  • Illegal Content & Dangerous Activities: Sale of illegal narcotics, weapons, fraud instructions, or cyberattacks.
  • Spam & Scams: Automated bulk uploads, phishing URLs, commercial affiliate spam, or deceptive cryptocurrency schemes.
  • Impersonation & Deceptive Profiles: Posing as an authentic individual or verified organization without parody disclosure.
  • Intellectual Property Infringement: Unauthorized distribution of copyrighted media without fair use justification.
  • Privacy Violations & Doxxing: Exposing non-public personal information, addresses, or telephone numbers without consent.

2. Enforcement & Account States

  • Content Hidden: Content removed from public feeds and search pending final review.
  • Content Removed: Media permanently blocked from retrieval and references cleared.
  • Account Restricted: Publishing suspended while browsing remains permitted.
  • Account Suspended / Banned: Complete revocation of UGC publishing and authenticated platform privileges.
