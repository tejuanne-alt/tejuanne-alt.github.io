MemeApp Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 | Version: 2026-10-v1

This Privacy Policy explains how MemeApp collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data when you access our mobile application and related web services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Email address, encrypted authentication tokens, and user ID when creating an account.
  • Profile Information: Username, display name, biographical text, and avatar image.
  • User-Generated Content: Uploaded meme images, captions, tags, and category classifications.
  • Interactions & Preferences: Meme likes, saved bookmarks, and user blocking records.
  • Safety & Moderation Data: Content reports submitted, incident details, and server-side policy acceptance records.
  • Device Media Access: Temporary photo gallery or camera access strictly when you select an image file to upload.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the public meme browsing and publishing feed.
  • To enforce Row-Level Security (RLS) policies and private storage authorization.
  • To protect user safety, investigate harassment/abuse reports, and enforce Community Guidelines.
  • To provide account settings and self-service permanent account deletion.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and personal data at any time directly in the mobile app settings or via our public Account Deletion Web Portal.

Upon deletion, all profiles, published memes, likes, bookmarks, and private media objects are permanently purged. Immutable moderation audit logs are retained strictly for safety, legal compliance, and abuse prevention purposes.

4. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding privacy, data protection, or legal compliance, contact our team at tejuanne@gmail.com or submit a ticket via our Public Support Portal.
